The Maker Behind the Mark

I design and build knives with a focus on geometry, balance, and real-world use. My work builds on a legacy started by my grandfather, grounded in respect for craftsmanship and purpose. I design digitally for precision and repeatability, then finish by hand to ensure each knife feels right in use. Technology allows the work to scale; hands and judgment keep it honest. Every decision, from grind to finish, is made with both performance and presentation in mind.

I build knives to be used, but also to be owned with pride. Some are meant for the field, some for daily carry, some to live on a workbench or in a collection. The goal isn’t to make one knife for one type of person. It’s to make knives for people who care, wherever and however they use them.

This brand is about growth. It’s about reaching more hands, more belts, more boots, more pockets. Scale and visibility matter, not as shortcuts, but as a way to put quality tools where they belong. As the brand grows, the range of knives will grow with it. If there isn’t a knife here for you yet, there will be.

The scorpion mark is a reminder of the core that doesn’t change. Resilience, precision, and respect for the environment and process that shape the work. No matter how far the brand goes, those values stay at the center.
